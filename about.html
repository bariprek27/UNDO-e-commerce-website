Our Core Philosophy

Good things deserve another round.

UNDO was conceived as an antidote to disposable fashion culture. We believe the most sustainable garment is the one that has already been made.

How It Operates

The Circular Lifecycle

From one wardrobe to another, without ending up in landfills.

01

Discovery

Community members inspect their closets for quality pieces they no longer wear and list them with transparent notes.

02

Transparent Curation

Each listing transparently details sizing, fabric feel, condition, and any signs of gentle wear so shoppers know exactly what they receive.

03

Rewearing & Love

A new owner styles the piece, giving it years of renewed utility and personal expression instead of fresh manufacturing impact.

04

Passing It Forward

When you're ready for a change, simply list the piece back onto UNDO for someone else to enjoy. The loop continues indefinitely.

Practical Longevity

The Garment Care Manifesto

Small daily habits make textiles endure for decades. Here are our essential principles for making pre-loved pieces last.

Wash Cold, Line Dry

High heat is the primary cause of fabric degradation, shrinking, and colour loss. Washing at 30°C or cold preserves denim fibres and delicate knits.

Visible Mending

A missing button, small tear, or scuff is not a reason to discard. Sashiko stitching, darning, and leather conditioning add authentic character.

Know Your Measurements

Vintage tag sizes often differ from modern vanity sizing. Compare actual chest, waist, and length measurements to eliminate return shipping waste.

Academic Context & Research Rationale

UNDO is created as a comprehensive Product Design group project. It investigates user trust in second-hand marketplaces, ergonomic thumb-reach in mobile commerce, WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility compliance, and honest circular-economy messaging without greenwashing.
